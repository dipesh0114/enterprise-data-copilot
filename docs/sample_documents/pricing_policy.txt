Pricing Policy Update - June 2025

Effective 1 July 2025, list prices for all Electronics products increase by 10 percent due to higher component costs from suppliers.

Enterprise customers in the South region were informed by email in May 2025. Several asked for volume discounts and some delayed purchase decisions until their annual budget review in October.

Furniture, Stationery and Appliances prices are unchanged for the rest of 2025.
